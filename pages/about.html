Meet Dr. Anna Ngo

Dr. Anna Ngo is the owner and optometrist at Sight Studio, where she provides comprehensive, personalized eye care in a comfortable and educational environment. She earned her Doctor of Optometry degree from the Chicago College of Optometry and has trained under experienced doctors across Connecticut, Massachusetts, Illinois, and Florida.

Dr. Ngo specializes in primary eye care and is passionate about building lasting relationships with her patients—caring for them and their families as their vision needs grow and change over time. She is deeply committed to patient education, ensuring that every visit is informative and empowering.

Fluent in English and Vietnamese, Dr. Ngo is dedicated to making quality eye care accessible and welcoming to all members of the community.
